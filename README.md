## What is make.fun?

Make.fun is a board of bounties — small competitions to build software, with real prize money in USDC, a dollar-backed token on Solana. You pick a bounty, build what it asks for with an AI agent, and submit it before the deadline. If your entry places, you claim the reward to your own Solana wallet.

The bounty I started with is called "Bring the humans again". It pays $1,000 in total, split across 50 places: $150 for 1st, $80 for 2nd, $50 for 3rd, $20 each for 4th to 10th, $17 each for 11th to 25th, and $13 each for 26th to 50th. For that one, the entry is a getting-started guide like this one, and the score is how many real people sign up through your referral link.

## What counts — and what doesn't

Only real people count. A signup counts when a new person creates an account in the competition window through your link, and the house's check verifies it. Your own account doesn't count. A bot doesn't count. An account that signs up and never does anything doesn't count. Each signup also waits through a 24-hour review hold before it can be verified, so bring people in early, and bring in people who actually want to try it.

## Sharing your link

Don't spam strangers with your link — that doesn't work, and bot or idle signups never count. Share it tonight, not tomorrow morning, with real friends who would actually want to try make.fun, and tell them what it is in your own words. If they sign up and take part, that is what counts. A short message that worked for me:

> I just joined make.fun — you build things and enter bounties for real prizes. I wrote a getting-started guide from my phone. If you want to try it, sign up with my link and give a bounty a go: https://make.fun/r/mgefsztg

## 1. Sign up

1. Open my link: https://make.fun/r/mgefsztg
2. Tap **Sign in**, then sign in with Google.
3. Pick your Gmail account. Google may ask you to approve the sign-in on your phone — it shows you a number, you tap **Yes**, then tap that number.

That was the fiddliest part for me. Google asked me to confirm my phone number, then sent a number to tap, then asked me to set a new password. Take it one screen at a time, and don't share any code Google sends you with anyone in a chat.

## 2. Finish your profile

1. Choose a username. Mine is `@duruaku-austin`.
2. Write one honest line for your bio. Mine is: "Austin — building with AI agents and exploring make.fun bounties. Learning in public, one entry at a time."
3. Add a picture if you have one. Mine is a generated image of a king, cropped square. A clear photo works too — PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 2 MB.

## 3. Link GitHub

Most bounties need GitHub, because your entry lives in a public GitHub repository.

1. On make.fun, open your profile, then the **Identity** tab.
2. Tap **Link GitHub**.
3. I sign in to GitHub with Google, so I choose the Google option there — I don't have a GitHub password, and I didn't need one.
4. GitHub may email you a one-time code to check your device. Find it in your Gmail, enter it, and approve it.
5. Turn on **"Show your GitHub on your profile"**.

My GitHub is `duruakuaustin4-dotcom`, and it shows on my profile now.

## 4. Link your Solana wallet — do this before you submit

For this bounty, a linked wallet is required to submit, not just to get paid. Your entry is signed by the wallet linked to your account, so link it before the deadline.

I use Solflare, and I do it on my phone. One thing to know on Android: Chrome on Android does not support extensions, so there is no Solflare extension to install in Chrome.

What worked for me: sign in to make.fun as yourself, go to your profile, **Identity** tab, tap **Link a wallet**, choose Solflare, and approve it in the Solflare app when it asks. My wallet is linked now, and it shows on my profile as my primary wallet.

Never share your seed phrase or private key — not with a website, not in a chat, not with an agent. Linking a wallet should never ask you for those words. If something does, stop.

## 5. Leave a real comment first

Before you build anything, open the bounty board, pick a bounty you actually like, and leave a real comment — a question about the task, or a thought about the rules. Not "nice project". It shows you read it, and it gets you started.

My first comment on this bounty asked whether a click today plus an account tomorrow, before the deadline, still counts, and whether the 24-hour review hold has to clear before the deadline. I was asking because the timing matters for sharing.

## 6. Pick your first bounty

Start with one you understand. I picked this guide because it is writing, not code. Other bounties on the board ask for a web app, a game, a video, or a post on another site. Read the bounty page first — what to build, the deadline, and what each paid place gets — and check whether you have the accounts or tools it needs before you start.

The board is sorted into Trending, New and Ending soon. The next bounty drops on Saturday morning, so check back even if nothing fits today.

## 7. Build it, check it, submit it

- Read `RULES.txt` in the bounty's kit. For a bounty, the rules in that file are the law — if a guide says something different, the rules win.
- Build in a fresh agent session that started after the bounty opened. Sessions that began before it cannot be entered.
- Write the entry in that session, with your own referral link and your own repository in it. Don't copy another entry — copied guides get rejected.
- Make a new public GitHub repository under the GitHub account linked to your make.fun account, after the bounty opens, with an open-source licence. Mine will carry an MIT licence.
- Put the repository's About website to the bounty page on make.fun, and the topic `makefun`.
- Once the repository is published, take a screenshot of its GitHub page with the README showing, and save it as `screenshot.png` at the root of the repository.
- Submit before the deadline. For "Bring the humans again" that is Saturday 21 November, 11:30 WAT. The last submission before the deadline is the one that counts.

## What I got wrong at first

- I thought the wallet could wait until I won. For this bounty it can't — it is needed to submit.
- I tried to link the wallet in a normal browser, and it said no wallet was found. On a phone, the wallet lives in the Solflare app, not in Chrome.
- I mixed up referral codes at first. The link I signed up *with* belonged to the friend who invited me. My *own* code is `mgefsztg`, and that is the one in this guide. Always check your own Referrals tab before you put a link anywhere.
